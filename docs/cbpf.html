Country sets:

Country-level SEAS5 seasonal precipitation alerts for every country. The 20 countries in the second tranche of the US “Humanitarian Reset” award are outlined in red; the 29 countries with an OCHA pooled-fund envelope (country-based or regional RHPF) are outlined in orange. Countries with no pooled fund are shown a little paler. Use the outline toggles to show or hide each set. Countries that are hard to see at this scale (Haiti, El Salvador, oPt, Lebanon, Fiji, Solomon Islands, Bangladesh, Guatemala, Honduras) are drawn as dots so their forecast value stays visible. Forecast issued —. Colours show where this SEAS5 forecast sits within its historical distribution, where the seasonal forecast has skill; hatched fills indicate moderate (vs. high) skill. Grey = outside the rainy season; cross-hatch = low skill. Use the Issued year/month menus to browse past forecasts (in-sample reconstructions). By default, alerts are shown only for the rainy season.

Data: ECMWF SEAS5 (forecast) & ERA5 (climatology). OCHA Centre for Humanitarian Data.
